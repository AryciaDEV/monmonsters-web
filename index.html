MonMonsters: free online monster game

Choose your starter monster and explore an online world with other players. MonMonsters runs directly in your browser, with no game installation and no advertisements.

How to play

Choose a starter when you first enter the game. Explore the world, complete quests, collect rewards and develop your monster collection. Discover mystery chests, hatch eggs in the incubator and use shrines for boosts.

Play on desktop or mobile

Open this page in a browser with WebGL support. On supported phones and tablets, use the on-screen touch controls. Keep an internet connection for online play and saved progress. The first visit downloads the game, so loading may take a little longer.

Are there ads?

No. MonMonsters has no video ads or advertising interruptions. Rewards can be claimed without watching ads; individual activities may still have cooldowns or daily limits.
